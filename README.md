percentchange.m - this script produces Figures 1 and 2 from our manuscript, depicting the (1) percent change in nymphal infection prevalence and (2) the absolute change in the density of infected nymphs as hosts are added or removed from a community.

varyingdensity.m - this script produces Figure3 from our manuscript, depicting nymph density as mice and squirrel density varies.

fragmentation.m - this script produces Figure 4 from our manuscript, depicting nymphal infection prevalence, the density of infected nymphs, and the density of nymphs as mice density increases.
